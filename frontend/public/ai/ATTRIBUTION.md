Human hologram portraits (2026-10-19)
-------------------------------------

`hologram-female.png` and `hologram-male.png` were created with Codex's built-in
image generation tool for this assistant. They depict synthetic adults, not
identified people. No existing person's likeness or third-party portrait was used.
The application samples these sources onto an animated WebGL point relief with
subtle parallax and audio-driven displacement. This is an image-derived relief,
not a scanned head or a fully rigged, view-independent digital human.
